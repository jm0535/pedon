Pedon Studio is an offline-first field companion for soil scientists, foresters, agronomists, and land managers.

Features:
• Describe soil profiles in the field using FAO-standard horizon codes
• Automatic WRB 2022 classification with 22 diagnostic calculators covering all 32 Reference Soil Groups
• Qualifier evaluation and final WRB name generation
• 19 field tools: texture, pH/salinity, bulk density, carbon stocks, CEC/base saturation, RUSLE erosion, lime requirement, and more
• Photo capture and compression
• GPS coordinate recording
• Export to PDF, Word (.docx), CSV, GeoJSON, or JSON backup
• Dark/light mode with manual toggle
• Works completely offline — no internet permission required
• All data stays on your device

WRB 2022 engine covers all 32 Reference Soil Groups: Histosols, Anthrosols, Technosols, Cryosols, Leptosols, Solonetzes, Vertisols, Solonchaks, Gleysols, Andosols, Podzols, Plinthosols, Planosols, Stagnosols, Nitisols, Ferralsols, Chernozems, Kastanozems, Phaeozems, Umbrisols, Durisols, Gypsisols, Calcisols, Retisols, Acrisols, Lixisols, Alisols, Luvisols, Cambisols, Fluvisols, Arenosols, and Regosols.

Reference: IUSS Working Group WRB. 2022. World Reference Base for Soil Resources. 4th edition, corrected 24 September 2024.
