Describe soils in the field. Classify with WRB 2022.

Pedon Studio is an offline-first field companion for soil scientists, foresters and agronomists. Record profiles, horizons, lab data, GPS and photos, then get an evidence-based WRB 2022 classification — no internet needed.

Features

Everything for the soil pit, in your pocket

WRB 2022 engine

22 diagnostic calculators covering all 32 Reference Soil Groups, with principal and supplementary qualifiers and full name construction.

Profile description

Site data, FAO horizon codes, Munsell colour, texture from sand/silt/clay, structure, consistence, mottles and pH.

Laboratory data

SOC, CEC, base saturation, ECe, CaCO₃, gypsum, bulk density, oxalate/dithionite Fe-Al-Si, phosphate retention and more.

19 field tools

Texture, carbon stocks, CEC/base saturation, RUSLE erosion, lime requirement, infiltration, soil moisture and more.

GPS and photos

Capture coordinates offline and attach compressed profile photos.

Exports

PDF and Word reports, CSV, GeoJSON for QGIS/ArcGIS, lab sample lists and full JSON backups.

Private by design

No internet permission, no accounts, no tracking. Your data stays on your device.

Dark and light mode

Follows your device theme, with a one-tap toggle for bright sunlight.
